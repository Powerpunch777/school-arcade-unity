# Gameplay media provenance

## Gameplay video

Recorded on **October 23, 2026 (Asia/Seoul)** from the supplied **CMP-265 Windows build**, with the project owner playing. The final video is a continuous **70-second** excerpt of real play, including the opening forest combat, the upgrade choice, selection of piercing arrows, and entry into the next room. It replaces the October 17 silent recording.

- [`gameplay.mp4`](../../docs/gameplay.mp4): H.264, 1280 × 720, 30 fps, normal playback speed, with AAC stereo audio at 48 kHz. The game's music and effects were recorded from speaker output alongside the screen; no microphone audio or replacement soundtrack was added. Audio was aligned to the picture, reduced by 5 dB, and given short fades at the beginning and end.
- [`gameplay-preview.gif`](../../docs/gameplay-preview.gif): a silent six-second preview from seconds 5–11 of the final video, resized to 640 × 360 at 12 fps. Open the full MP4 to hear audio.
- [`poster.jpg`](../../docs/poster.jpg): a frame from second 13 of the final video.
- [`captions.en.vtt`](../../docs/captions.en.vtt) and [`captions.ko.vtt`](../../docs/captions.ko.vtt): explanatory captions added for viewers; these are not game dialogue or transcribed audio.
- [Watch the video](https://powerpunch777.github.io/school-arcade-unity/): a simple player hosted with GitHub Pages from the repository's `/docs` directory.

The footage uses the normal game, without automated QA scenes or changes to the build. Menu time and footage outside this excerpt were omitted. The clip documents the opening room and the start of the next encounter, not a full campaign playthrough. Upgrade choices differ from the separate screenshot session below.

Captured on **October 10, 2026 (Asia/Seoul)** by launching `SoulArcher-CMP-265.exe` and using the normal game menus, with Easy difficulty selected. These are application-window captures; they are not concept art, mockups, or images from an automated QA scene.
