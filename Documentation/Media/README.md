# Gameplay media provenance

## Gameplay video

Recorded on **October 17, 2026 (Asia/Seoul)** from the supplied **CMP-265 Windows build**. The final video is a continuous **34-second** excerpt of real play in the opening forest room, including movement, combat, the upgrade choice, and selection of piercing arrows.

- [`gameplay.mp4`](../../docs/gameplay.mp4): H.264, 1280 × 720, 30 fps, normal playback speed, no audio. Only the game footage is included.
- [`gameplay-preview.gif`](../../docs/gameplay-preview.gif): a six-second preview from seconds 10–16 of the final video, resized to 640 × 360 at 12 fps.
- [`poster.jpg`](../../docs/poster.jpg): a frame from second 9 of the final video.
- [`captions.en.vtt`](../../docs/captions.en.vtt) and [`captions.ko.vtt`](../../docs/captions.ko.vtt): explanatory captions added for viewers; these are not game dialogue or transcribed audio.
- [Watch the video](https://powerpunch777.github.io/school-arcade-unity/): a simple player hosted with GitHub Pages from the repository's `/docs` directory.

The footage uses the normal game, without automated QA scenes or changes to the build. Menu time and footage outside this excerpt were omitted. The clip documents the opening room, not a full campaign playthrough. Upgrade choices differ from the separate screenshot session below.
